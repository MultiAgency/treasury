# The web builds

`web/` is its own package in the pnpm workspace: React 19, TanStack Router and Query, rsbuild, Tailwind 4 and shadcn, on `@multiagency/sdk` 0.5.5 only. Nothing is imported from the platform's or the prototype's source. rsbuild builds the app's pages.

## The app's pages (`src/`)

The build (`src/`, built to `dist/`), served by the app server, not the platform: the owner signs in to that server with their wallet, connects the agent with a consent, then approves or rejects each proposal by signing its exact text and executes approved ones. `src/server-api.ts` is its only module that calls a server, parsing every answer with `apps/server/src/contract.ts`. It has pages, from one table (`src/pages.ts`) that its TanStack Router routes and its titles are built from (#129): `/` your treasuries, `/setup/dao/<dao>`, and an agent's `/agents/<agent>/` (Home: what needs your decision, what happened since you last looked, where the agent stands, its book and portfolio, and its connection; creating it while it isn't), `…/proposals`, `…/proposals/<id>`, `…/strategy`, `…/funding`, `…/activity` (the full history, the owner's actions, the autopilot's runs at `…/activity/runs/<id>`, and the books) and `…/ai` (Agents: the stabilization agent and the NEAR agent), each with one `<h1>`, inside a frame with the agent's name, who is signed in and the navigation. An address that names nothing says so. The server serves the page for every path, so each address opens directly.

It is the one Treasury app, decisions first (#128). The owner-session build it replaced was removed in #138 ([ADR 2](adr/0002-owner-ui-app-access.md)).

## Building and testing

```sh
pnpm --filter @treasury/web build       # web/dist, the app's pages
pnpm --filter @treasury/web typecheck
pnpm --filter @treasury/web test        # unit tests; the API modules against a mocked fetch
pnpm --filter @treasury/web test:stack  # builds, then the app's pages' two stack tests
```

The app's pages are tested through the app server, run in-process with the stack's own `tap_` credential: `test/app.e2e.ts` on a stack with a wallet-owned agent, the owner's wallet signing through the stack's `signOwner` control, and `test/dao.e2e.ts` on one with a DAO-owned agent, its council signing with their own key and the DAO voting through the stack's `daoVote` control. Both scan the pages they open with axe (WCAG A and AA).

- **Forward risk** (on the agent's Home and `/strategy`, #220): the stabilization agent's latest recorded assessment, read from `GET …/risk` (the server's audit log, never a platform call): the forward level with its reasons, the projected drawdown, expected shortfall, chance of breaching the cash floor and months to it, the three most severe stress scenarios, and what the agent would do (hold buys, de-risk with its size and whether it was proposed, pause autopilot, consider a freeze). The de-risk links to the Proposals page where the owner decides it; a freeze is worded as a recommendation the owner signs, never as something the agent did. Without a record the card says no assessment has been recorded yet. It is on both pages because the level that holds or slows the strategy's trades belongs beside the strategy, and the owner's first read is the Home page. The view's wording is `src/risk-view.ts`, unit-tested under Node; the pages themselves are scanned with axe (WCAG A and AA) by the stack tests.
- **Agents** (`/agents/<agent>/ai`, #219, #220): the two agents the owner can talk to. The **stabilization agent** shows whether this server runs it and how often, its latest assessment in full (the same card as Home and Strategy), **what it has been doing** (its recent assessments from `GET …/risk/history`, newest first: level, why, what it recommended, whether it proposed a de-risk), a **what-if** (an event and a value, validated before anything is sent, run over its recorded book by `POST …/risk/what-if` and shown as today beside after, with the cash floor and whether it could trade), and a conversation held to forward risk. The **NEAR agent** is the treasury assistant inline, with the whole app in reach. Its drafts open the review that already exists (rebalance, strategy, policy, grant, freeze, and the Strategy page's autopilot controls) or go to a page; the owner signs, never the agent. The conversations are kept while the owner moves between pages (`useConversation` in `assistant.tsx`, per agent and persona) and shared with the top bar's sheet. Where the server has no model the conversations say so and the records stay readable. The view's wording is `agents-view.ts`, unit-tested under Node; the page is scanned with axe by the stack test.
- **Strategy proposals** (on `/agents/<agent>/strategy`, #206): the strategies the agent's proposers suggest, each beside the saved strategy term by term (targets, swap limits, autopilot, cash floor), with who proposed it and why (`strategy-proposals.tsx`, its comparison in `strategy-proposals-view.ts`). Adopting one, behind a confirm dialog, saves it as an edit would; the Authorize step then asks for the grant that binds it, a DAO's by its vote. Declining closes it. Every save sends the saved cash floor with it, since the platform saves a strategy whole. On the stack, the DAO's e2e adopts one and files its grant vote.
- **Treasury assistant** (the top bar of every agent page, #219): a sheet to ask about the treasury in plain language, shown only where the server answers (`GET /api/assistant`). An answer is text only, with each figure's source shown as an `S1` mark and each source listed with the record it came from and when it was read; a draft card lists what it changes and opens the existing flow filled in (the rebalance form, the strategy editor, the policy editor, the grant review, or the freeze review where the owner is). The draft is held in memory for one agent until its flow takes it (`assistant-drafts.ts`), and the flow says it was filled in by the assistant; nothing is sent or signed by opening it. The conversation stays on the page while the owner moves between the agent's pages, and earlier turns go back to the server as text only. The page's e2e asks a question through the deterministic `rules` provider, follows a draft into the strategy editor, and scans the open panel with axe.
- **The authorize dialog** (the grant's review on `/strategy`, #260): shows the strategy's terms being authorized (targets, swap limits, autopilot's interval and ceiling, and the cash floor, with `StrategyTerms`) above the digest the grant binds, for a wallet owner's signature and for a DAO member filing it for the vote.
- **Who has voted on a DAO request** (`dao-request.tsx`, #262): once the DAO's proposal for a request is filed, the page lists the accounts that approved it, as Sputnik records them (the server passes `approvedBy` on the request), beside the threshold the platform reports for the DAO (`dao.approvers` on the `treasury` read, read on the proposal page), quoted as given in `dao-votes-view.ts` (unit-tested under Node). The app does not decide whether the threshold is met: that is the DAO's and the platform's to say. *Needs your decision* shows a count of approvals so far.
- **Home's first-run and plain-words pass** (`/agents/<agent>/`, #254 items 1 to 2, #258, #259): *Where the agent stands* adds **Funds** (whether the overview's managed book holds anything, public or confidential, with a dollar total only when every holding is priced) and, for a wallet owner only, **Account** (whether a funding account is linked, from the `treasury` read; a DAO's is the DAO, fixed at setup, so it has no cell); each links to Funding. Their wording is `standing-view.ts`, unit-tested under Node. The policy's "Not in force" reads **Blocked** (on Home and in the Policy page's figures, keeping the platform's reason under it), the approve-only host's strategy status reads **Approve-only**, and an operation without a proven outcome reads **Pending**: on Home it is listed under its own "Pending, no action needed" heading, apart from *Needs your decision* and not counted in "waiting", because the platform settles it on its own; on Activity the section is titled "Pending". Item text says what happened and that the platform is checking it.
- **Funding leads with one way in** (`/agents/<agent>/funding`, #261): which way leads is `leadWay` in `funding-view.ts` (unit-tested under Node). A linked account leads with **Money in** from it (for a DAO, the Trezu proposal); a confidential DAO leads with paying from its confidential balance, its public balance going under **Other ways to fund the agent**; with no account linked and the owner able to link one, linking leads (*Use {account}* is one click); with none linked and none linkable, the one-time deposit address leads. **Receive funds** is under *Other ways* whenever it doesn't lead. Shielding public balances is not a way in and stays its own card below. The linked account and its unlink control stay on their own card.
- **Ask why** (on a proposal's page and the forward-risk card, #264): opens the treasury assistant with a question in its box (`ask-why.ts`): the proposal's names its ID, so the assistant reads it with `get_proposal`; the risk card's asks about the risk level, answered from `get_risk_report`. The question is only filled in: the owner reads it and sends it, and opening it sends nothing. The button is shown only where the assistant answers (`useAssistantStatus`).
- **Since you last looked** (on Home, #263): the autopilot's runs, the proposals made and the risk assessments after the owner's last visit, using the existing `runs`, `proposals` and `riskHistory` endpoints. Home reuses its proposals query and adds one runs read and one risk-history read; these do not poll or add endpoints. The summary is written in words (`since-view.ts`, unit-tested under Node). The last visit is kept in this browser's `localStorage`, per account and agent, and written when the owner leaves Home or the page, using the visit start only after all three reads succeeded freshly (cached or failed reads retain the prior cutoff); nothing is kept on the server. With no earlier visit, or storage that can't be read, it covers the last seven days and says so; a visit time in the future is treated as none. With nothing to report the card is not shown.
- **Policy** (`/agents/<agent>/policy`, #134, #142): the policy as signed, its limits beside what is reported left, the editor (reusing `policy-draft.ts` and `policy-review.ts`, now in `src/`), the grants with revocation behind a confirm dialog, and the revisions with the mandate digest, collapsed. Issuing or renewing a grant stays on the Strategy page; the proposal page's `policy_not_in_force` refusal links here. On the stack, the page's e2e revokes the grant by keyboard, signed once, and reads it ended (#187).
- **Diversification** (`/agents/<agent>/strategy`, #221): the diversified book from `GET …/diversification` (`diversification.tsx`, its rows in `diversification-view.ts`), one `overview` read when the section is shown. Each holding is named with its class, chain, route and every venue its path crosses, in the asset colours (#193); each venue shows its exposure and cap with where the cap came from (the agent's configured mandate terms, the profile's defaults, or absent); the stablecoins show their share, target and peg level; and each phase shows On/Off with every platform reason it can't run (BTC not a proposal asset, no second stablecoin, no tokenized T-bill). The section says the caps are app-side: the platform's signed policy still has the last word. The rows carry text, not colour alone, and each table has a caption and scoped headers.
